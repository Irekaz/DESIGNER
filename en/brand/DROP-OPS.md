# DESIGNER — Drop Ops

## Site architecture
| State | What the user sees |
|---|---|
| Idle | Brand hero + waitlist + archive of previous drop (sold out) |
| Teaser | Colour pairs, 1–2 sample images, countdown |
| Open | Active SKUs only with cap counter; pre-order checkout |
| Closed (floor met) | “In production” + delivery estimate |
| Closed (floor not met) | 1 extension OR full refund |
| Shipped | Archive “DROP 01 — sold out” |

**No** always-on catalogue. **No** wholesale in v1.

## Shopify setup (recommended)
- Theme: minimal, brand-first
- Apps: Preorder / Incoming (or Shopify Checkout preorder), Klaviyo / email waitlist, hard inventory cap
- Inventory = cap (nothing more)
- When 0: sold out — do not restock the same SKU / colourway
- Payments: Stripe; Vipps via Norwegian Stripe / Shopify where possible
- Visible delivery estimate, e.g. “Ships 8–12 weeks after drop closes”

Bib shorts → Drop 02 (spring).

## Production floor (set after factory quote)
Example: min 40 Loop Shirts OR economic break-even after sample cost. Document the floor before the drop opens.

## Payment (Drop 01 recommendation)
**100% pre-order** at purchase (simplest cashflow).  
Alternative: 50% now + 50% 14 days before ship (more friction).

## Legal copy (draft — have counsel / accountant approve)
- Pre-order for a limited drop; production starts when the floor is met
- Estimated delivery: X–Y weeks after drop close
- Withdrawal rights after receipt under Norwegian / EU consumer law
- If floor is not met: full refund within Z days
- No guarantee of restock for the same colourway

## Ambassador allocation
8–12 slots reserved before public open (not an evergreen discount code).

## KPIs — Drop 01
- ≥ 70% of cap sold
- Floor met without cancellation
- Gross margin ≥ 60% after shipping
- Returns &lt; 15% (bib &lt; 12%)
- Margin covers Drop 02 samples
