# DESIGNER — Visual Brief, Colour Card & Pattern Rules

**Drop 01 chapter:** *Ochre Band* / *Lime Band*  
**Formula:** `Minimal silhouette + limited surface + bold accent + smart reflectivity`

## Moodboard direction (words)

| Layer | Reference | Take | Don't take |
|---|---|---|---|
| Silhouette | Pas Normal Off-Race, quiet techwear | Clean lines, few panels | Race neon, sponsor look |
| Colour energy | [Vlisco](https://vlisco.com) Super-Wax / Sky Wax | Saturated accents, contrast, luxury signal | All-over wax print, figurative clichés |
| Texture idea | Wax crackle / bubble marks | Subtle texture in print or reflective ink | Literal batik costume |
| Context photo | Carbon road bike, city-edge, café after the ride | Expensive bike in frame without a price tag | Watt / Strava screens |

### Reflective
| Type | Spec |
|---|---|
| Reflective print / piping | Silver / glass-bead or equivalent; follows pattern geometry |
| Visibility | Subtle in daylight; clear in car headlights |
| Zones | Rear hem, rear pocket edge, upper arm; bib: rear thigh / hem |

**Forbidden:** Fluoro-yellow hi-vis, purple gradients, cream + terracotta “AI default”, multi-shadow glow.

## Pattern rules (DESIGNER Motif Family v1)

**Name:** *Band Geometry* — abstracted bands/arcs inspired by wax-print graphics, not figurative motifs.

1. One motif family per drop chapter.
2. Max **one** accent zone per garment (rear pocket panel OR cuff band OR side stripe).
3. Jersey: max one band or rear panel; rest solid.
4. No all-over print in Drop 01.
5. Motif drawn original for DESIGNER — **no Vlisco assets**.
6. Reflective must **reuse the same geometry** (same curves/lines).

### Placement map
```
Loop Shirt:  [solid body] + [rear pocket panel OR cuff band] + [reflect hem]
Jersey:      [solid body] + [rear pocket band OR side stripe] + [reflect hem/pocket edge]
Bib:         [solid] + [gripper accent colour] + [reflect rear thigh]
```

## Typography / wordmark
- The word **DESIGNER** is the hero signal (landing + hangtag).
- Expressive grotesque or hybrid display — **not** Inter / Roboto / Arial / system.
- Slightly open tracking; all-caps OK on hangtag.
- On-garment size: discreet neck / hem — the brand speaks through colour/motif, not a chest logo.

## Photo rules (drop teaser)
1. Full-bleed city ride / road-edge
2. Product on body in motion
3. At least one car-light / night shot for reflectivity
4. No floating badges / promo chips
5. Brand name visible in the first viewport on site
